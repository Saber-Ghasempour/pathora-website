Pathora Puzzle Support

Need a hand finding your path?

Email pathora@afsanora.com with your app version, device model and a description of the issue. Screenshots are welcome; avoid including account details, passwords or sensitive information.

Saved progress

Ordinary puzzles work offline. Use Sync saved game in the achievements panel when connected. On iPhone and iPad, check Game Center sign-in and iCloud Drive. Android uses Google Play Games. These platforms do not share an account.

Optional rewards

Rewarded videos currently use test ads. A reward is granted only after the ad confirms completion; closing early may not grant one. If no ad loads, you can continue ordinary play or restart.

Puzzle invitations

Open a shared link from Messages or Notes, or paste its code into Open a shared puzzle in the game. Make sure both players use an up-to-date version.
