PATHORA / AFSANORA

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026 · Last updated: 11 October 2026

This policy explains how Afsanora handles information in Pathora and on this website. Pathora is designed for teens aged 13 and over and adults; it is not directed to children under 13.

It covers Pathora for Android (package com.afsanora.pathora), including Google Play Games features introduced in version 1.0.2, and the browser version where available. Earlier Android versions and the browser version keep game progress locally and do not include this Google Play Games integration. Contact: pathora@afsanora.com.

The short version

Pathora saves progress on your device for offline play. On Android 1.0.2 and later, a connected Google Play Games profile enables automatic cloud saves and achievement reporting through Google. We do not operate a separate Pathora account or cloud-save server, or sell personal information. Android version 1.0.4 introduces optional rewarded test ads in closed testing; the browser game has no ads. Google processes information through its services as described below.

1. Information stored on your device

Pathora stores completed stages, unfinished campaign and Free Play puzzles, remaining arrows, hearts and hints, elapsed times, daily completion dates, best records, achievements and preferences such as theme, sound, vibration, motion and board settings. This supports continuing a puzzle and playing offline. Stages and daily puzzles are generated or loaded locally; they are not downloaded from an Afsanora server.

2. Google Play Games, cloud saves and achievements

On supported Android versions, the Google Play Games SDK attempts to authenticate your Play Games profile according to your Google settings. When authentication succeeds, Pathora attempts to synchronize from the main menu automatically. There is no separate sync button. Offline play remains available. Conflicting unfinished puzzles may require you to choose which puzzle to keep.

Cloud synchronization sends completed stages, unfinished campaign and Free Play puzzles, their remaining arrows, hearts, hints, elapsed times and board position, daily completion dates and times, best records, earned achievements and completed tutorial flags to Google's Saved Games service. These saves are associated with your Play Games profile. Achievement unlocks are also reported to Google. A Play Games player identifier is kept locally to prevent mixing saves belonging to different accounts. Pathora does not store your Google password or request your email address, contacts or friends list for these features.

Google's SDK processes gamer identity and service information, as well as analytics and diagnostics for service stability and improvement. Achievement visibility to other people depends on your Play Games profile settings. Google encrypts Play Games Services data in transit using HTTPS. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Play Games Services data disclosures. Google controls retention of data held by its services, subject to your account controls.

3. Optional rewarded ads, analytics and permissions

Android version 1.0.4 in closed testing uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK with Google's official demo ad identifiers. These are test ads and generate no advertising revenue. Earlier releases without this feature and the browser game do not show ads. There are no banner ads or automatic interstitial ads. You can choose an ad for one extra heart (once per puzzle attempt) or three extra hints when the relevant offer is available. Restarting and ordinary play do not require watching an ad.

Before your first ad request, a local date-of-birth screen determines an age-treatment category. The full date is used only in memory, then cleared; it is not saved, included in cloud saves or sent to Google. Only the treatment category is saved on your device. The app sends age-treatment flags to the ad SDK. If you cancel this screen, the app does not initialize the ad SDK or request an ad.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK processes IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions such as taps and video views, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers such as app set IDs for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Demo ads still involve SDK network requests; they are not a promise of zero data collection. This build removes the Android advertising-ID permission and disables publisher first-party ID use. Other SDK processing may still occur. Ad requests use a maximum content rating of G and disable advertising personalization.

Google states that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. See Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure, Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps. Google controls retention and processing within its services; we do not receive a separate copy of SDK device-level records.

The closed-test build uses Google's demo configuration and does not display a publisher consent form. Live advertising has not been enabled. Before enabling live inventory, we will configure and test the applicable consent messages and privacy controls and update this policy if data handling changes.

We do not add Google Analytics for Firebase, enabled Unity Analytics or cloud crash reporting. This does not exclude processing by the Google SDKs described here. Pathora does not request precise location, microphone, camera or personal photo-library access. Internet access supports Google Play Games and optional ads; vibration provides optional feedback. Google Play may also provide aggregated store and quality reports.

4. If you contact us

When you email pathora@afsanora.com, we receive your email address, message and any attachments. We use these to answer your request and investigate issues. Our email provider processes messages for delivery. Please do not send passwords or sensitive information. We retain correspondence only as long as needed for support, follow-up and applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion by contacting us.

5. This website and hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub and its providers may process IP addresses, requested URLs, browser information and request times to deliver and secure the site. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for its processing and retention practices.

We do not add advertising trackers, analytics scripts, contact forms or marketing cookies to this website. Images and styles are served with the site. External links and email services have their own privacy practices. The browser game, where available, keeps its progress in browser storage.

6. Sharing information

Cloud saves and achievement information are sent to Google when Play Games features are used. When you choose an optional ad, information is also processed by Google as described in section 3. Support messages and website requests are processed by the providers described above. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information where required by law or to protect the security and legal rights of our users and services.

7. Your choices and deletion

You can decline optional ad offers and continue ordinary play or restart a puzzle. The local age-treatment category is removed when you clear app storage; it is not restored by Pathora cloud synchronization. Deleting local data does not delete information already processed by Google. See Google's privacy controls and contact details in the policies linked above for that information.

Manage automatic sign-in and the account used for Pathora through your Google Play Games settings. You can play locally without connecting a Play Games profile. Cloud synchronization needs a connection and a linked profile. Sound, theme, vibration and motion preferences are available in the game.

Clear Android app storage or uninstall to remove local game data. Clear the site's browser storage to remove browser-game progress. Device backups and Google cloud saves may remain after local deletion and must be managed separately.

To delete Pathora data held by Play Games, follow Google's instructions for deleting Play Games data and select Pathora. Deleting Play Games data does not necessarily delete a copy on your device. A remaining local copy can be uploaded again on a later sync; stop automatic sign-in and remove local copies and relevant backups as well if you want a complete reset.

There is no separate Pathora account to delete. For information you sent directly to Afsanora, or to request access, correction, deletion or other privacy rights available where you live, email pathora@afsanora.com.

8. Intended audience and younger users

Pathora has no in-game account registration or chat. For optional ads, users identified as under 18 receive child-directed treatment as a conservative setting; no ad is requested before an age-treatment category is known. All ad requests use the G content-rating limit and personalization is disabled. The age screen determines ad privacy treatment; it does not block access to the game. Applying protective ad settings to younger users does not change our intended audience of ages 13 and over. Google Play Games features are subject to Google's account and age requirements. We do not knowingly ask children to send us personal information. If you believe a child sent personal information to Afsanora through support, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

9. Security and changes

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the information we handle, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. We update this policy when features or data handling change and provide additional notices or choices where required.
