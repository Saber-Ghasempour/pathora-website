PATHORA / AFSANORA

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026 · Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy explains how Afsanora handles information in Pathora and on this website. Pathora is a general-audience puzzle game. Pathora Puzzle for iOS/iPadOS uses a 4+ content rating and is not listed in Apple's Made for Kids category. The current Android release remains targeted to ages 13 and over while support for a younger Google Play audience is reviewed.

It covers Pathora for Android and Pathora Puzzle for iOS/iPadOS (package and bundle identifier com.afsanora.pathora), including Google Play Games features introduced in version 1.0.2, and the browser version where available. The iOS beta adds Game Center and iCloud features in version 1.0.12. Earlier Android versions and the browser version keep game progress locally and do not include this Google Play Games integration. Contact: pathora@afsanora.com.

The short version

Pathora saves progress on your device for offline play. On Android 1.0.2 and later, a connected Google Play Games profile enables automatic cloud saves and achievement reporting through Google. We do not operate a separate Pathora account or cloud-save server, or sell personal information. On iOS, Game Center and iCloud support saved progress and achievements. Android and iOS test builds include optional rewarded demo ads; the browser game has no ads. Google and Apple process information through their respective services as described below.

1. Information stored on your device

Pathora stores completed stages, unfinished campaign and Free Play puzzles, remaining arrows, hearts and hints, elapsed times, daily completion dates, best records, achievements, earned coins, coin spending records, selected help, winning-streak bonuses and preferences such as theme, sound, vibration, motion and board settings. This supports continuing a puzzle and playing offline. Stages and daily puzzles are generated or loaded locally; they are not downloaded from an Afsanora server.

Monthly artwork unlocks are based on your saved daily completion dates. Tutorial dismissal settings and collected artwork references are also stored locally.

Sharing a puzzle: If you choose Share or Copy, Pathora creates a message containing the puzzle settings, generator version and seed in a link, plus the displayed time, hearts lost and hints used. It does not include your Google or Apple account identifier. You choose the receiving app and recipient; their services handle any message you send. Opening the link visits our website unless the operating system opens it directly in Pathora. Afsanora does not operate a shared-puzzle leaderboard or store these results in a separate game database.

2. Google Play Games, cloud saves and achievements

On supported Android versions, the Google Play Games SDK attempts to authenticate your Play Games profile according to your Google settings. When authentication succeeds, Pathora attempts to synchronize from the main menu automatically. You can also request synchronization from the in-game achievements panel. Offline play remains available. Conflicting unfinished puzzles may require you to choose which puzzle to keep.

Cloud synchronization sends completed stages, unfinished campaign and Free Play puzzles, their remaining arrows, hearts, hints, elapsed times and board position, daily completion dates and times, best records, earned achievements, coins and spending records, purchased help, winning-streak state and completed tutorial flags to Google's Saved Games service. These saves are associated with your Play Games profile. Achievement unlocks are also reported to Google. A Play Games player identifier is kept locally to prevent mixing saves belonging to different accounts. Pathora does not store your Google password or request your email address, contacts or friends list for these features.

Google's SDK processes gamer identity and service information, as well as analytics and diagnostics for service stability and improvement. Achievement visibility to other people depends on your Play Games profile settings. Google encrypts Play Games Services data in transit using HTTPS. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Play Games Services data disclosures. Google controls retention of data held by its services, subject to your account controls.

Apple Game Center and iCloud (iOS/iPadOS)

Pathora Puzzle uses Game Center authentication and iCloud saved games to synchronize progress between Apple devices. This requires Game Center sign-in and iCloud Drive. Saves contain the gameplay information described above, including coins and help purchases; they do not contain payment-card information. Synchronization can run from the main menu or when you choose Sync saved game. Conflicting saves require a choice rather than adding coin balances together. Android and Apple saves are separate; there is no cross-platform Pathora account.

The app keeps an account-binding value based on the Game Center player identifier and a hash of the iCloud identity token to avoid mixing saves from different accounts. These identifiers are used for save ownership, not sent in puzzle invitations. Pathora does not request your Apple password, email, contacts or friends list. Completed achievements are reported to Game Center; visibility is controlled by your Apple settings. Apple processes account and game activity under its own policies. See Game Center & Privacy and Apple Privacy. We do not operate an Afsanora cloud-save server.

3. Optional rewarded ads, analytics and permissions

Android closed-test versions starting with 1.0.4 and iOS beta versions starting with 1.0.12 use the Google Mobile Ads SDK with Google's official demo ad identifiers. These are test ads and generate no advertising revenue. Earlier releases without this feature and the browser game do not show ads. There are no banner ads or automatic interstitial ads. You can choose an ad for one extra heart (once per puzzle attempt) or three extra hints when the relevant offer is available. Version 1.0.8 also offers a rewarded video to start a Zen puzzle with unlimited hearts and hints. Restarting and ordinary play do not require watching an ad.

From version 1.0.7, Pathora does not ask for or collect your date of birth or age. Every optional ad request uses the same conservative child-restricted treatment, regardless of the player's actual age. This is an ad-request setting, not an assessment of your age. Earlier test versions used a date entered in memory to derive a local treatment category; they did not save or transmit the full date. Version 1.0.7 removes that legacy category from local storage on startup. The ad SDK is initialized only after you choose to request an optional rewarded ad.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK processes IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions such as taps and video views, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers such as app set IDs for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Demo ads still involve SDK network requests; they are not a promise of zero data collection. On Android, this build removes the advertising-ID permission and disables publisher first-party ID use. Other SDK processing may still occur. Ad requests use a maximum content rating of G and disable advertising personalization.

On iOS, Google reports that the Mobile Ads SDK may process IP-derived approximate location, diagnostics, performance data, device identifiers, advertising data and app/ad interactions. The current app does not request App Tracking Transparency authorization or intentionally access IDFA. This does not imply that other SDK data is not processed. See Google Mobile Ads iOS data disclosures.

Google states that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. See Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure, Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps. Google controls retention and processing within its services; we do not receive a separate copy of SDK device-level records.

The closed-test build uses Google's demo configuration and does not display a publisher consent form. Live advertising has not been enabled. Before enabling live inventory, we will configure and test the applicable consent messages and privacy controls and update this policy if data handling changes.

We do not add Google Analytics for Firebase, enabled Unity Analytics or cloud crash reporting. This does not exclude processing by the Google SDKs described here. Pathora does not request precise location, microphone, camera or personal photo-library access. Internet access supports Google Play Games, Game Center/iCloud and optional ads; vibration provides optional feedback. Google Play and Apple may provide store and quality reports. TestFlight may provide feedback and diagnostic reports through Apple; information you choose to submit is handled through that service.

4. If you contact us

When you email pathora@afsanora.com, we receive your email address, message and any attachments. We use these to answer your request and investigate issues. Our email provider processes messages for delivery. Please do not send passwords or sensitive information. We retain correspondence only as long as needed for support, follow-up and applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion by contacting us.

5. This website and hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub and its providers may process IP addresses, requested URLs, browser information and request times to deliver and secure the site. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for its processing and retention practices.

We do not add advertising trackers, analytics scripts, contact forms or marketing cookies to this website. Images and styles are served with the site. External links and email services have their own privacy practices. The browser game, where available, keeps its progress in browser storage.

6. Sharing information

Cloud saves and achievement information are sent to Google when Play Games features are used, or to Apple when Game Center/iCloud features are used. When you choose an optional ad, information is also processed by Google as described in section 3. Support messages and website requests are processed by the providers described above. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information where required by law or to protect the security and legal rights of our users and services.

7. Your choices and deletion

You can decline optional ad offers and continue ordinary play or restart a puzzle. Deleting local data does not delete information already processed by Google or Apple. See the providers' privacy controls and contact details in the policies linked above for that information.

Manage automatic sign-in and the account used for Pathora through your Google Play Games settings. You can play locally without connecting a Play Games profile. Cloud synchronization needs a connection and a linked profile. Sound, theme, vibration and motion preferences are available in the game.

On iOS/iPadOS, manage Game Center sign-in and activity sharing in system settings. Manage Pathora iCloud data through your Apple account storage controls where available. Deleting the app removes local app data; offloading can retain it. iCloud saves and device backups may remain and must be managed separately. A remaining local or cloud copy can restore progress after reinstalling or syncing. Sign out of Game Center or disable the relevant iCloud access before removing all copies if you want to prevent re-upload. Apple controls retention of information held by its services.

Clear Android app storage or uninstall to remove local game data. Clear the site's browser storage to remove browser-game progress. Device backups and Google cloud saves may remain after local deletion and must be managed separately.

To delete Pathora data held by Play Games, follow Google's instructions for deleting Play Games data and select Pathora. Deleting Play Games data does not necessarily delete a copy on your device. A remaining local copy can be uploaded again on a later sync; stop automatic sign-in and remove local copies and relevant backups as well if you want a complete reset.

There is no separate Pathora account to delete. For information you sent directly to Afsanora, or to request access, correction, deletion or other privacy rights available where you live, email pathora@afsanora.com.

8. Intended audience and younger users

Pathora has no in-game account registration or chat. Optional ads use conservative child-restricted treatment for all players. All ad requests use the G content-rating limit and personalization is disabled. We do not show an age or date-of-birth form. These settings apply to every player, without inferring their age. The iOS/iPadOS 4+ content rating describes content suitability; it is not age verification or parental consent. We have not yet expanded the Android store target audience to children under 13 or declared Google Play Families readiness. Google Play Games and Apple Game Center/iCloud features are subject to their providers' account and age requirements. Younger players should ask a parent or guardian for help with optional online services, sharing and support. Children should not send personal information to Afsanora through support; a parent or guardian can contact us instead. If you believe a child sent personal information to Afsanora through support, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

9. Security and changes

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the information we handle, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. We update this policy when features or data handling change and provide additional notices or choices where required.
