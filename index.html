GOOD TO KNOW

A few clear answers.

Who is Pathora designed for?

Pathora is designed for teens aged 13 and over and adults who enjoy spatial puzzles and planning ahead. It is not directed to children under 13.

Can I play without an internet connection?

Yes. The current Android game generates its stages and daily puzzles locally. Ordinary campaign, daily and Free Play puzzles work offline after installation. Optional rewarded ads, including starting a new Zen puzzle, require an internet connection.

Do I need an account?

You do not need a separate Pathora account to play. With Google Play Games integration: when signed in through Google, progress syncs automatically from the main menu and earned achievements are reported to Google. Local play remains available without a connection. Manage automatic sign-in through your Play Games settings.

What happens if I leave a puzzle?

Continue stages returns you to your unfinished campaign stage. Free Play has a separate saved puzzle that you can resume from its settings screen.

Does Pathora have ads?

The latest Android closed-test build includes optional rewarded test ads for one extra heart, three extra hints, or starting a Zen puzzle. There are no banners or automatic ad breaks. One heart revival is available per puzzle attempt. These demo ads do not generate revenue; an internet connection is needed. Ordinary play remains available offline. See our Privacy Policy for SDK data handling.

How can I send feedback?

Email pathora@afsanora.com. For technical issues, your device model, Android version and a description of what happened can help us investigate. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information.
